### Role description

Architects and CAD Designers produce 2D drawings, 3D models and large design files, often with several windows or multiple monitors and frequent design reviews. Their software needs fast CPU cores, a dedicated GPU, plenty of RAM, fast storage and generous screen space, so their workstations need to be stronger than the office builds but do not need the extreme rendering power of the 3D Visualisation role.
